== Description ==

[PLACEHOLDER: the full description is written in round six.]

OmnaBase for WordPress connects your site to your OmnaBase workspace in one approval:

* Your support team sees the orders, subscriptions and licences of a customer who bought on your site (WooCommerce, Easy Digital Downloads, License Manager for WooCommerce), from the sources you switch on.
* Topics in the bbPress forums you mark arrive as tickets, and your team's answers appear as forum replies.
* A shortcode form sends questions straight to your inbox.
* Logged-in customers read and answer their own tickets on your site.

== External services ==

[PLACEHOLDER: completed in round six, with the privacy text.]

This plugin connects to **OmnaBase** (https://app.omnabase.com), a service of Kriesi Media GmbH, once an administrator connects the site. It sends what the features you turn on need: forum topics and replies in the forums you mark, form messages, the customer data sources you switch on, and licence changes. It receives the replies and status changes of your own site's tickets. Nothing is sent before you connect.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin's zip under Plugins › Add New › Upload Plugin, and activate it.
2. Open Settings › OmnaBase.
3. [PLACEHOLDER, round one] Click Connect and approve the connection on OmnaBase.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does my site need https? =

Yes. OmnaBase delivers replies and asks for customer data over https on the standard port, so the site needs a public https address.
